# Floor Plan (web)

Measure a room with a phone's camera and tilt sensors, and export a simple floor plan as a PDF.

Open the page on a phone, hold the phone at a known height, aim the cross at each floor corner and mark it.
Everything runs in the browser on the phone. The page has no network code: nothing you measure is uploaded.

This is an early prototype. It measures one room at a time and is only as accurate as the phone height you enter.
