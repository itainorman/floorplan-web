Floor Plan

Measure a room with the camera and the phone's tilt sensors. Everything stays on this phone: nothing is uploaded.

  1. Stand near the middle of the room and stay on that spot.
  2. Aim the cross exactly where two walls meet the floor, then tap Mark corner. Aiming even a little up the wall makes the room come out much too big. If furniture hides a corner, aim at the floor spot where the corner would be.
  3. Turn on the spot and mark each corner in order around the room.
  4. Tap Finish to see the plan.
Every distance is worked out from this number. If it is wrong, the whole plan comes out too big or too small. You can fix it afterwards: measure one wall with a tape and tap that wall's length on the plan.
Your phone will ask for two permissions Motion & Orientation, to know where the phone is pointing, and Camera, to show what you are aiming at. Tap Allow for both. Neither the picture nor the measurements are recorded or sent anywhere.
